EYLog Observation Exporter

Safely download your child's learning journey, photos, and videos.

Note: This tool requires a desktop computer and a modern browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Brave). It will not work on mobile phones or Safari due to how it saves large files directly to your hard drive.
1

Show your Bookmarks Bar

If you can't see your bookmarks bar under the web address at the top of your screen, press Ctrl + Shift + B (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + B (Mac) to show it.

2

Drag the button to your Bookmarks

Click and hold the yellow button below, drag it up to your bookmarks bar, and let go. It will create a new bookmark called "EYLog Exporter".

↪ EYLog Exporter

(Drag me to your bookmarks bar!)

3

Run the Exporter

Go to your EYLog Parent Portal and log in. Once you are on the dashboard, click the "EYLog Exporter" bookmark you just created.

4

Save your file

A popup box will appear in the top right. Select which child you want to export using the dropdown, and click Start Export. It will ask where you want to save the ZIP file on your computer. Pick a folder, click Save, and watch the progress box in the top right corner.

Privacy Note: This script runs entirely within your own web browser. It does not send your data, passwords, or photos to any third-party servers.
